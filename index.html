Sliderz – Privacy Policy

Last updated:30 November 2025

Sliderz is developed by Maxime Derzelle. This privacy policy explains how the application handles user data.

1. Information We Collect

Sliderz uses Google Firebase services (including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) to help us understand how the game is used and to improve stability and performance. These services may collect certain information automatically, such as:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App information (app version, build number)
  • Usage information (screens visited, basic interaction events)
  • IP address (used to derive approximate location, such as country or region)
  • Crash information (error messages, stack traces, and related technical data)

This data is collected in an anonymized or pseudonymized form where possible and is used only for analytics and debugging purposes. We do not use this data to personally identify individual users.

2. Local Data Storage

Sliderz also stores minimal game-related data locally on your device, such as:

  • Level progression
  • Stars or accomplishments earned
  • Editor unlock status

This locally stored data is used solely to support gameplay features and is not transmitted directly by the app outside of the telemetry described above.

3. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Analyze gameplay patterns and improve level design and difficulty balance
  • Identify and fix crashes, bugs, and performance issues
  • Improve overall user experience and stability

We do not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising in the demo version of Sliderz.

4. Third-Party Services

Sliderz uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics

These services are provided by Google and operate according to their own privacy policies. For more details on how Google and Firebase handle data, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

5. Children’s Privacy

Sliderz does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The data collected through Firebase is limited to technical and usage information for analytics and crash reporting only. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, please contact us so that we can delete the information.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the application evolves, especially if new features (such as additional online services or ads) are introduced. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
