Sliderz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Sliderz (the “Game”) is a puzzle game developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you play Sliderz, including its community features.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Analytics and crash reports

Sliderz uses Google Firebase services to understand how the Game is used, identify technical problems, and improve the player experience. Depending on the SDK configuration, these services may collect:

  • Device information, such as device model and operating system version.
  • App performance and diagnostic information.
  • Crash reports and technical error details.
  • Gameplay events and general usage statistics.
  • App instance identifiers and other identifiers used by Firebase services.

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for these purposes.

1.2 Anonymous player accounts

Sliderz uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to identify players without requiring a name, email address, or password. Firebase assigns each anonymous account a unique user identifier (UID). We may use this identifier to associate published levels with their creators, track likes and prevent duplicate voting, manage reports and moderation, and protect community features against misuse.

Although players do not need to provide contact information, anonymous account identifiers may still be personal data under applicable privacy laws.

1.3 Community levels

Players may create and publish puzzle levels that other players can discover and play. When a level is published, we may store its structure and gameplay configuration, unique identifier, creator UID, publication date, recorded move count or other gameplay metadata, likes, and information necessary to validate or moderate the level.

Published level layouts and associated public gameplay information are visible to other players. Sliderz does not offer free-form text messaging, voice chat, or direct image or audio uploads through its community features.

1.4 Likes and reports

We may store which levels a player has liked, reports of inappropriate levels, the account identifier associated with those actions, and moderation decisions. Reports and moderation records are not intended to be publicly visible.

2. Local Data Storage

Sliderz may store level progression, settings, locally created levels, and other gameplay information on your device. This information generally stays on the device unless you use a feature that uploads it, such as publishing a community level. Uninstalling the Game may remove local data, which may not be recoverable after reinstalling or changing devices.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and maintain Sliderz; publish, discover, and rate community levels; associate content with anonymous accounts; validate and moderate content; prevent abuse; diagnose technical problems; improve gameplay; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell players’ personal data. The initial demo contains no advertisements or in-app purchases.

4. Community Content and Moderation

Published levels are available to other players. Players must follow the Game’s Community Guidelines when publishing content. We may remove content that violates those guidelines, including illegal, abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, or otherwise inappropriate content. We may restrict community access in cases of repeated misuse.

Players can report inappropriate levels in the Game. Reports may be reviewed manually and may involve information associated with the reported level and its creator.

5. Third-Party Services

Sliderz uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication (anonymous player identification), Cloud Firestore (community data), Cloud Functions (server-side validation and moderation), Firebase Analytics (usage statistics), and Firebase Crashlytics (diagnostics).

These services may process information on Google infrastructure under their applicable terms and privacy practices. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect player information. Firebase communications use encrypted connections, and access to community data is controlled through backend authorization and security rules. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain community safety, meet legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described here. Players may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Deletion may include the anonymous authentication account, player-specific community records, published levels, and likes or other account-linked activity, as applicable. Certain information may be retained where legally permitted or required for security, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.

8. Children’s Privacy

Sliderz is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to provide the Game’s features, subject to applicable legal requirements. If we learn that information has been collected or processed contrary to applicable children’s privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns involving minors.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive certain personal data; object to certain processing; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may need to verify that a request relates to the correct anonymous account.

Users in the European Economic Area may also lodge a complaint with their competent data protection authority.

10. International Data Processing

Firebase information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, we rely on applicable safeguards for international transfers of personal data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its community features, legal requirements, or service providers change. The updated version will be published here with a revised “Last updated” date.
